# Go-to-Market & Pricing Strategy
## Recruitin Lead Generator Ecosystem

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Dit document beschrijft de go-to-market strategie en pricing voor het Recruitin lead generator ecosysteem, bestaande uit:

1. **The Prompt Gym** - Gratis AI training game voor recruiters
2. **Kandidatentekort** - Lead magnet/tool voor schaarste-inzichten
3. **RecruitmentAPK** - Assessment/audit tool voor recruitment teams

### Kernstrategie
**"Free Value → Paid Expertise"** - Bied gratis tools die directe waarde leveren, verzamel leads, en converteer naar betaalde workshops en consulting.

## 💰 Pricing Strategy

### Tier 1: Gratis Lead Magnets

| Product | Doel | Lead Value |
|---------|------|------------|
| **Prompt Gym** | AI skills testen + templates unlocken | €15-25/lead |
| **Kandidatentekort Tool** | Schaarste data + marktinzicht | €20-30/lead |
| **RecruitmentAPK** | Team assessment + verbeterpunten | €30-50/lead |

**Strategie**: Hoe meer waarde de gratis tool geeft, hoe hoger de lead kwaliteit en conversie naar betaald.

### Tier 2: Low-Ticket Producten (€47-€197)

| Product | Prijs | Inhoud |
|---------|-------|--------|
| **Prompt Template Bundel** | €47 | 50+ ready-to-use prompts |
| **AI Recruitment Playbook** | €97 | Complete gids + video's |
| **Sourcing Masterclass** | €147 | 3-uur on-demand training |
| **Complete Template Vault** | €197 | Alle templates + updates |

**Doel**: Snelle conversie van leads, bewijs van betalingsbereidheid

### Tier 3: Workshops (€297-€997)

| Workshop | Prijs | Format | Capaciteit |
|----------|-------|--------|------------|
| **AI Prompt Mastery** | €297 | 3 uur live | 20 deelnemers |
| **Advanced Sourcing** | €497 | Dag workshop | 15 deelnemers |
| **AI Recruitment Bootcamp** | €997 | 2 dagen | 12 deelnemers |

**Scarcity Tactics**:
- Maximum 15-20 deelnemers per sessie
- Early bird pricing (€50-100 korting)
- Countdown timers op landing pages
- "Nog X plekken beschikbaar" messaging

### Tier 4: Enterprise & Consulting (€2.500+)

| Service | Prijs | Inhoud |
|---------|-------|--------|
| **Team Training On-Site** | €2.500/dag | Maatwerktraining voor teams |
| **AI Implementation** | €5.000+ | Setup + training + support |
| **Recruitment Transformation** | €10.000+ | Complete AI-integratie |

## 🔗 Cross-Selling Strategy

### Lead Flow Between Products

```
Prompt Gym User
    │
    ├─── Completes game ──→ Offer: "Check je kandidatentekort"
    │
    ├─── Downloads templates ──→ Offer: "Doe de RecruitmentAPK"
    │
    └─── All engaged ──→ Offer: "Volg de complete workshop"

Kandidatentekort User
    │
    ├─── Gets report ──→ Offer: "Train je AI skills"
    │
    └─── High schaarste score ──→ Offer: "Workshop: AI Sourcing"

RecruitmentAPK User
    │
    ├─── Low AI score ──→ Offer: "Start met Prompt Gym"
    │
    └─── High improvement potential ──→ Offer: "Team Training"
```

### Email Sequence Strategy

**Prompt Gym Completers**:
1. Day 0: Bedankt + templates download
2. Day 2: "3 prompts die je nog niet kent"
3. Day 5: Case study: resultaten met AI
4. Day 7: Workshop invite (early bird)
5. Day 10: Urgency: "Nog 5 dagen early bird"
6. Day 14: Last chance email

**Cross-Product Nurturing**:
- Na elke tool completion: introduce andere tools
- Segment op basis van engagement
- Personaliseer op basis van interesses
